| The second line under medicines and conditions where it said what they do ("can induce hypoglycemia", "blunts adrenergic hypo symptoms", "alters HbA1c glycation dynamics") | Uncited health claims (rules.md 1.9). Example and brand names are kept, to help the person recognise theirs |
