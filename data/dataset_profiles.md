## CGMacros (manifest #5)

One CSV per person, one row per minute: Libre glucose, Dexcom glucose (mg/dL, to be converted to mmol/L), Fitbit heart rate and activity, and meals with macros and a photo path. `bio.csv` has age, sex, BMI, HbA1c, fasting labs and three fingersticks. Licence CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (non-commercial, share-alike, attribution).

- **45 participants**, 7.2 to 19.2 days each; HbA1c 4.6 to 8.5.
- HbA1c under 5.7: 15, 5.7 to 6.4: 16, **6.5 or more: 14**. The 6.5 line is our assumption, not a label in the dataset.
- **14 of the 14 with HbA1c 6.5 or more have complete data** (glucose in at least 70% of minutes, at least 10 meals, at least 5 days). Subjects: 3, 5, 12, 14, 28, 30, 35, 36, 38, 39, 42, 46, 47, 49.
- Among those: 22 to 46 meals, heart rate present 76% to 99% of minutes, 36 to 92 meal photos.

Watch out: dates are shifted; a person has 7 to 19 days, so there is no long history; US foods only, no sodium column.

## 250k Medicines (Kaggle, manifest #20)

- 248,218 rows x 58 columns: id, name, 5 substitute, 42 sideEffect, 5 use columns, and Chemical Class, Habit Forming, Therapeutic Class, Action Class.
- **There is no interactions column and no active-ingredient column.** The clash checker cannot use this file. Decision (owner, via Joely): use the openFDA drug-label `drug_interactions` text instead.
- Rows with at least one use listed: 100%; with at least one side effect: 100%.
- Rows whose name contains 'metformin': 69. Names are brand names with strength and form (for example 'augmentin 625 duo tablet'); mapping to generic names has to come from elsewhere (openFDA or RxNorm).
- **Decision: the five `substitute*` columns are dropped when loading** (a substitute could look like medication advice). The raw file keeps them.

Watch out: source is given only as 'various sources', brand names may not match Singapore, not authoritative, no dosing.

## Open Food Facts (Kaggle, manifest #17)

- 356,027 products (163 columns; profiled: name, country, energy, carbs, sugars, fibre, salt, sodium).
- **Singapore: 58 products; Malaysia: 17; either: 75.**
- In the Singapore/Malaysia subset: sodium or salt present for 14 (19%), sugars present for 15%, name present for 71%.
- Target from the backlog (E1-4): at least 90% of seeded foods have sodium or are flagged unknown.

Watch out: packaged products only (not hawker dishes) and values per 100 g. **The Singapore/Malaysia subset is far too thin to be the whole `foods` table**: most rows lack sodium, so unknown values must be flagged unknown (the LLM estimates cover local dishes, D-011).

## Doctor Specialty (Kaggle, manifest #37)

Files: Disease_Description.csv, Doctor_Specialist.csv, Doctor_Versus_Disease.csv, Original_Dataset.csv, Specialist.xlsx, Symptom_Weights.csv.

- `Doctor_Versus_Disease.csv`: **41 rows, no header, 2 columns (disease, specialty).** This is the only file we load, and only the disease-to-specialty pairs.
- Never load or index the symptom tables (`Symptom_Weights.csv`, `Original_Dataset.csv`): symptom-to-disease is diagnosis.

Watch out: weak provenance; use only for conditions the patient already has (routing, not diagnosis).
